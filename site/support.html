Where the time goes.

Building

New features

Everything on this site started as an evening's idea: the score bug, multiview, Tally Pulse, the Samsung and LG apps, commentary language. Support buys more of those evenings.

Testing

Real TVs and real servers

Releases are tested against all three Jellyfin lines, 10.10, 10.11 and 12.1, and on TVs, phones and streaming boxes, because a fix on one can break another.

Keeping up

Wholphin and Jellyfin updates

Tally follows Wholphin's releases and every new Jellyfin version, so it keeps getting their improvements and keeps working after your server updates.

Help

Answering issues

Reading logs, reproducing problems on the same hardware, and writing guides like the ones on this site.

02Other ways to help

No money needed.

  • Report bugs well. A clear report with a log is worth more than you'd think. See below.
  • Tell a friend who runs Jellyfin. Word of mouth is how an app outside the stores gets found.
  • Star the projects on GitHub: Tally, and Wholphin, which Tally wouldn't exist without.
  • Contribute. Pull requests are welcome; the repository's CONTRIBUTING guide explains how Tally keeps its changes to Wholphin small.
03Reporting bugs

How to report a bug.

Open an issue on GitHub. Please report Tally problems to Tally, not to Wholphin or Jellyfin, even if the problem looks like theirs: we'll pass it on if it is.

  1. Check the FAQ first

    Many common problems have a fix in troubleshooting.

  2. Say what you have

    The device (Shield, Fire TV Stick 4K, Samsung 2022, Pixel 8…), the Tally version (Settings → About), the plugin version and the Jellyfin version (Dashboard).

  3. Say what happened

    What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot or a phone photo of the TV helps.

  4. Attach a log

    • Server and live TV: the lines around the problem from Jellyfin's log (log_*.log). Live channel lines start with JellyTV.
    • Samsung or LG installer: Tally-Samsung-Installer.log or Tally-LG-Installer.log from the computer's temp folder.
    • App crash: say yes when Tally offers to send the report; it goes to your own Jellyfin server, where the admin can find it.

    Remove anything private first, like your server's public address or stream links.
