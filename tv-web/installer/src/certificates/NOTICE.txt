Third-party material in Tally for Samsung (Tally-Samsung-Installer)
===================================================================

Tizen's public signing material (not carried in the program)
  The "Tizen Developers CA" with its key (password in Tizen Studio's conf.ini) and Tizen's public distributor
  ("Tizen Public Distributor Signer", tizen-distributor-signer.p12, password "tizenpkcs12passfordsigner") are
  downloaded on first use from Tizen's server, in Tizen Studio's certificate generator package
  (https://download.tizen.org/sdk/tizenstudio/official/binary/certificate-generator_0.1.4_ubuntu-64.zip, checked
  against a pinned SHA-256), and kept in the program's app-data folder. Source: Tizen's certificate generator,
  https://git.tizen.org/cgit/sdk/tools/certificate-generator/, Copyright (c) 2012 - 2013 Samsung Electronics Co.,
  Ltd., licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt). The same files ship with every
  Tizen Studio.

samsung/ - Samsung's public CA certificates for TV developer certificates
  vd_tizen_dev_author_ca.pem ("Samsung VD Author CA"), vd_tizen_dev_public2.pem ("VD DEVELOPER Public CA
  Class"), vd_tizen_dev_partner2.pem ("VD DEVELOPER Partner CA Class"): public certificates, used only to complete
  the chain of certificates Samsung's service issues. From Tizen Studio's Samsung Certificate Extension 2.0.75
  (res/ca/, Apache License 2.0 per its feature license) and published by Samsung in
  https://github.com/Samsung/tizen-agent-skills (tizen-sdk-skills/common/assets/samsung-tv-ca/, Apache License 2.0).
  Stored as PEM, otherwise unmodified.
